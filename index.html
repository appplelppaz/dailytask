Shifts tonight's start time earlier or later, keeping the length and order of the tasks as they are. It applies to tonight only — tomorrow goes back to the default (19:30 on weekdays, 20:00 at the weekend).
Tapping the screen pauses the run, and tapping again resumes it. Whatever time is spent paused pushes the later tasks back by the same amount.

The pictures are photographs posted to Unsplash, served by Lorem Picsum, and they change every three seconds while a task is running. They are only fetched while a task is actually running, and if there is no connection the screen simply stays dark.
